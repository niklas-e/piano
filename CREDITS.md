# Credits

## Piano sound

The piano in `public/samples/piano/` is the
[Salamander Grand Piano V3](https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3)
by Alexander Holm, licensed under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), as packaged for
[Strudel](https://strudel.cc/) in
[dough-samples](https://github.com/felixroos/dough-samples).

One recording every minor third, from A0 to C8, is used unchanged and
repitched for the keys in between.
